## Why these three

- **fence** — a railing above a blank concrete wall. Adeia's whole idea is a
  fence you set, and the lower two-thirds of the frame is empty, so an overlaid
  panel has somewhere to sit without covering the subject.
- **limit-line** — one hard diagonal shadow across grey concrete. Reads as a
  threshold drawn on a surface. Abstract enough to use as a band without
  competing with type.
- **passage** — a concrete corridor lit from above, running into dark. Sits on
  the `--ink` ground; the dark end gives display type somewhere to land.
